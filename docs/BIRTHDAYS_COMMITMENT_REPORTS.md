# Automatic birthdays and commitment reports

Applied migration `202610130007_birthdays_work_insights.sql` and the birthday cleanup operation to live Supabase on 13 October 2026 after explicit approval.

Dashboard birthdays are generated from active employees' saved dates. Everyone with an active account sees names and calendar birthday dates, never birth years. Upcoming birthdays cover seven days. Birthday messages can be sent on the birthday date, survive refreshes, and become unreadable exactly 24 hours after creation. The private `birthday_messages` table has no client read/write grants. Author identity is taken from the signed-in profile, with message limits and a five-second send throttle. The active pg_cron job `birthday-messages-expiry` runs every minute and removes expired rows. This means physical removal can occur up to one minute after expiry. Birthday cards from the previous day remain visible only while an unexpired message remains. Existing image announcements remain supported; their old midnight-only wishes UI was removed.

Reports and Performance use assignment commitments/submission timestamps and timer intervals. Green means completed at or before the deadline, red means late completion or an unfinished assignment past its deadline, orange means an unfinished assignment on a currently held project. Missing commitment/submission data is not invented. Historical holds are not reconstructed: project status is current. The selected end date determines the deadline snapshot; inclusive calendar ranges are converted to exclusive IST midnight boundaries. Hours include project-only sessions, exclude pauses/manual timesheets, are clipped to the reporting window, and stop at the session's IST midnight. One assignment appears once; project time is not duplicated across tasks.

Filters: IST from/through dates, Today/This month/Last month, team, employee and outcome. Views: employee contribution, task commitments, dated timer log and projects. CSV exports task commitment results. All visible staff are available even with no work. Employee/team/project access boundaries are enforced by the database, independently of the UI filters; no sales privileges change.

Validation: 136 tests passed with bounded concurrency, TypeScript and production build passed. New database tests cover birthday privacy/expiry, send limits, staff/lead timer scope and timer clipping; outcome tests cover equality at deadline, lateness, holds, missing commitment and historical end-date behavior.
