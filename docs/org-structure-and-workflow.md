## Production Management & Project Coordination

- **Muskan** (`Muskan Kumari S`) - **Production Lead / Associate Lead** (Oversees entire production flow)

- **Esther** (`Esther`) - **Project Coordinator** (Takes client calls, plans & assigns video editing/designs to team members)

### Operational Workflow Sequence:
1. **Client Call**: Existing/new client calls Project Coordinator (Lavanya, Esther, Muskan) with requirements (e.g. video editing, graphic design, web dev, digital marketing).
2. **Response & Planning**: Project Coordinator accepts requirements, plans daily or weekly schedule for the team.
3. **Task Assignment**: Project Coordinator assigns projects/tasks to designated team members.
4. **Day Planner & Execution**: Day Planner / Timesheet starts for employees to execute assigned tasks.
5. **Team Monitoring**: Team Associate Leads (Ganesh for Graphic Design, Sudeesh for Video Editing, Vijay for Web Dev, Janani for Digital Marketing) monitor their respective team's progress.
