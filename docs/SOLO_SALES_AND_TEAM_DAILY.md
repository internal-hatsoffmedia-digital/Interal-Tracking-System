# Solo project sales and team daily overview

A completed, priced project with exactly one verified active contributor receives one sales-credit snapshot for that person. Contributors are unique linked profiles on non-rejected assignments of non-archived tasks. The project coordinator's final Completed status is authoritative for revenue; task/assignment statuses remain visible separately. No names are hardcoded and archived test assignments do not count as contributors.

Projects with multiple contributors remain subject to explicit leadership allocation. Existing manual credit records (including zero-value revocations) are preserved. Repeating completion does not duplicate a credit; reopening hides the project's credits until completion again. Amounts and the contributor's team are snapshotted when credited. Historical changes to assignments or team membership do not automatically redistribute an existing credit. Rate/share changes after completion require leadership review of the existing allocation.

Associate Leads and Team Leads get **Your team · Work & sales** on Dashboard: Today / This month / custom IST dates, employee filter, credited-sales totals, sales by completion date, work status table and a link to My Team activity/timing. The database derives the viewer's team from their authenticated profile. There is no team-ID parameter, and staff cannot call this team-lead RPC. This expands only own-team dashboard visibility; the company Sales page remains restricted to its existing leadership roles.
